---
title: "Payment plans"
description: "Group multiple independent payments under one review and one World ID approval."
---

Use a payment plan when one user wants to authorize several independently
settled payments together. A plan does not combine recipients, balances, or
funding instructions; every item remains a normal payment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a draft plan">
    Call `create_payment_plan` with a stable request ID and concise description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Estimate every item">
    Create one recipient-free estimate per payment. Reuse each unexpired
    result while its economic inputs are unchanged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve every recipient">
    For each fiat destination, choose one instrument from its estimate's
    `recipient_requirements`, then select or create the final recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the complete batch">
    Show every recipient, source and destination amount, fee, route, expiry,
    and material warning. Obtain one explicit confirmation for the complete
    plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add plan-bound payments">
    Call `create_payment` once per item using the shared `plan_id`, a unique
    `plan_position`, and a unique request ID. Do not set standalone
    `confirmed_by_user` on a plan-bound item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Call `review_payment_plan`, verify all intended items and positions, then
    call `submit_payment_plan` with `confirmed_by_user=true`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and continue each payment">
    Complete the single plan approval when required. Then track and continue
    every ready child payment independently.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Submission permanently seals the plan. Do not add, remove, or modify an item
after submission. Cancel the plan before funds move if the user abandons it.
A child payment can be continued concurrently, sequentially, or later, but
its provider and wallet constraints still apply.
