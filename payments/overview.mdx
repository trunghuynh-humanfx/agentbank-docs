title: "Overview"
description: "Understand durable AgentBank payments, tracking, corrections, and recovery."
---

An AgentBank payment is a durable record that connects route selection,
authorization, current execution instructions, and final status. The payment
record remains authoritative across browser actions, fiat settlement, and
on-chain execution.

<Note>
An on-chain transaction receipt does not replace aggregate payment status.
Use `get_payment` to confirm whether the complete AgentBank payment finished.
</Note>
