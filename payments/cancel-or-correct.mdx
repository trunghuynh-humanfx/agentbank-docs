---
title: "Cancel or correct a payment"
description: "Use the supported intervention path for a cancellable or recipient-paused payment."
---

## Cancel a payment

Obtain explicit user confirmation before `cancel_payment`, even though the tool
does not include a separate confirmation boolean. Cancellation is valid only
in supported states; do not promise that an executing or irreversible transfer
can be stopped.

## Correct a recipient

Use `correct_payment_recipient` only when the durable payment is paused in
`recipient_correction_required`. Collect canonical replacement details, show
them to the user, obtain confirmation, and resume from the returned state.

<Warning>
Do not create a second payment as a substitute for correcting a paused
recipient unless the original payment has reached a terminal state and a new
route is explicitly required.
</Warning>
