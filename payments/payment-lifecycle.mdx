---
title: "Payment lifecycle"
description: "Understand ephemeral estimates, durable payments, instructions, execution, and final status."
---

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    E["Estimate ready"] --> C["Create durable payment"]
    C --> A{"Authorization required?"}
    A -->|"Yes"| W["World ID authorization"]
    A -->|"No"| R["Approval ready"]
    W --> R
    R --> N["Continue payment"]
    N --> F{"Funding type"}
    F -->|"Fiat"| H["Payer completes instruction"]
    F -->|"Wallet"| X["Execute current instruction"]
    H --> P["Core tracks payment"]
    X --> P
    P --> T{"Terminal state"}
    T --> D["Completed"]
    T --> K["Cancelled"]
    T --> L["Failed"]
```

An estimate is temporary and has no ID. `create_payment` persists the request,
locks its route, and creates the authorization state. `continue_payment` opens
approved settlement steps and returns the current instruction.

`execute_payment_instruction` can execute only that current instruction through
the pinned wallet. `get_transaction_receipt` reports on-chain state, while
`get_payment` reports the authoritative aggregate payment state.
