---
title: "Track payments"
description: "Resume durable payments safely and report the authoritative state."
---

Use `get_payment` for one known payment and `list_payments` to find earlier
payments visible to the current local installation or hosted OAuth connection.

## Tracking rules

- Honor `next_action.poll_after_seconds` when returned.
- Show the user the current status and required action.
- Reuse the existing browser action while it remains current.
- Do not create a replacement payment because funding or authorization is
  pending.
- A completed wallet transaction is not final completion.
- For two-step routes, one completed step is not the completed payment.

Current payment statuses include `approval_required`, `approval_ready`,
`funding_required`, `funding_detecting`, `processing`, `need_review`,
`recipient_correction_required`, `completed`, `cancelled`, and `failed`.

Terminal states also include `expired` and `funding_timeout`. On hosted OAuth,
`get_payment` remains card-free: reopen a missed pre-funding instruction with
`get_payment_instruction`, and display `show_payment_progress` only after funds
move.
