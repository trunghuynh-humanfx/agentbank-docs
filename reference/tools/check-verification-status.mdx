**Authentication:** Valid connected-agent credential  
**Mutates state:** No

```json
{}
```

KYC states include `none`, `pending`, `in_review`, `verified`, `rejected`,
`expired`, and `abandoned`.

Market readiness is keyed by country and uses `APPROVED`, `PENDING`,
`NOT_STARTED`, or `REJECTED`. Current market mappings include ARS, BRL, IDR,
NGN, PEN, PHP, THB, and VND.

This readiness view does not prove that a live quote exists. Use
`list_quote_book_pairs` and `browse_quote_book` for live availability.
