---
title: "Limitations"
description: "Understand the explicit non-capabilities of the current AgentBank MCP surface."
---

- No automatic Core planner selects a settlement asset for two-step routes.
- Estimates are ephemeral, have no ID, and are not matched by
  `create_payment`.
- Expired or rejected payment authorization cannot be refreshed in place.
- Partial two-step failure has no dedicated automated recovery action.
- QR images are decoded, but text-only screenshot OCR is not implemented.
- Recipient updates create replacements and do not revoke the old record.
- No generic arbitrary EVM transaction tool is exposed.
- The payment status model does not emit `expired`.
- Wallet chain reads use one configured RPC endpoint.
- Partner identity and partner-query APIs are hidden.
- Legacy low-level settlement workflows are outside the production surface.
- A pending AgentKit verification session may need to be recreated after MCP
  restart.
- Shareable single-payment links are coming soon.
- The agent can track whether a collection was paid, not who paid it.
