---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Answers about custody, wallets, identity, autonomy, staging, collections, and payment status."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does AgentBank custody my funds?">
    AgentBank does not take custody of the user's AgentBank wallet funds. Privy
    provides wallet infrastructure. Payment partners may process funds during a
    supported route.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who controls the wallet?">
    The wallet is tied to the user's login. The user and a connected agent
    authorized by that user can operate it through granted AgentBank workflows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my agent transact automatically?">
    Yes, below the threshold configured at `app.agentbank.world` and within the
    agent's granted permissions. Above-threshold payments require World ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does staging work?">
    Create a supported staging on-ramp. It automatically succeeds and credits
    mock tokens; no faucet or real bank action is required.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can another person pay a collection?">
    Yes. Give the payer the order-specific bank account or QR instructions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the agent see who paid?">
    No. The current agent-facing state shows whether the order was paid, not
    payer identity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are payment links available?">
    Shareable single-payment links are coming soon. They are not currently part
    of the public tool surface.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a transaction hash final completion?">
    No. Use `get_payment` for the authoritative aggregate payment state.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
