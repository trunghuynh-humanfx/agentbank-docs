Connect AgentBank to Claude using a remote custom connector. Claude connects
through its cloud infrastructure; you do not need to run the local AgentBank
MCP package on your computer.

Claude supports custom remote connectors on Free, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise
plans. Free accounts are limited to one custom connector. The individual setup
steps below follow Claude's published Pro and Max instructions; Free users can
check the [current connector guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11175166-get-started-with-custom-connectors-using-remote-mcp)
for setup details available to their account.

## Add AgentBank

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open connectors">
    For an individual Pro or Max account, open **Customize → Connectors**,
    select **+**, then **Add custom connector**.

    For a Team or Enterprise workspace, an Owner first adds the connector in
    **Organization settings → Connectors → Add → Custom → Web**. Members then
    find AgentBank in **Customize → Connectors** and connect individually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Remote MCP URL">
    If your workspace Owner has already added AgentBank, skip this step and
    proceed to **Connect your AgentBank account** below.

    Name the connector **AgentBank** and enter:

    ```text
    https://plugin.agentbank.world/mcp
    ```

    Select **Add** to save the connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AgentBank account">
    Select **Connect** and complete AgentBank's OAuth sign-in in the browser.
    Choose the account you want Claude to access and review its permissions.
    Each person authorizes their own AgentBank connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable AgentBank in a conversation">
    Open a conversation, select **+ → Connectors**, and enable AgentBank.
    Ask:

    ```text
    Check my AgentBank account, permissions, and wallet readiness. Do not create a payment.
    ```

    The assistant checks `whoami`, `check_my_scopes`, and `list_wallets`.
    Confirm that the connected account is the one you intended to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use AgentBank

Ask Claude to preview a payment, prepare a payment plan, collect money, or
track a payment. Supported clients can display AgentBank's interactive cards
for review, approval, funding, and progress. See
[Remote MCP](/getting-started/hosted-oauth) for example requests and the
approval and funding flow.

Claude Desktop also supports account-based remote connectors. This is separate
from its local MCP configuration; follow [Connect your agent](/getting-started/connect-your-agent)
if you want the local stdio setup instead.

## Troubleshooting

- **Cannot add a custom connector:** check your plan and workspace permissions.
  Team and Enterprise Owners add connectors before members can connect.
- **Connection fails:** verify the complete URL, including `/mcp`, and retry
  the browser sign-in.
- **AgentBank is not available in chat:** enable it under **+ → Connectors**
  for that conversation.
- **A card is missing:** ask Claude to reopen the existing payment's card or
  provide the available action link.

For current availability and interface details, see Claude's
[custom connector guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11175166-get-started-with-custom-connectors-using-remote-mcp).
