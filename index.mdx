AgentBank web app, sign in, and chat directly with the hosted AgentBank agent
about what you want to do. No external AI client, MCP configuration, or skill
installation is required.

## MCP, skill, and Core

AgentBank separates tools, operating guidance, and durable execution:

- **AgentBank MCP** gives an agent authenticated tools and live account state.
- **AgentBank Pay skill** teaches the agent safe workflow order, confirmation,
  routing, tracking, and recovery.
- **AgentBank Core** authorizes the connected agent, locks payment routes,
  issues current payment instructions, verifies transactions, and stores
  durable payment state.

## You remain in control

Your AgentBank wallet is tied to your login and uses Privy wallet
infrastructure. Only you and an agent you authorize can operate it through
AgentBank. Configure an automatic transaction threshold at
[app.agentbank.world](https://app.agentbank.world): payments below the threshold
may proceed automatically within granted permissions, while payments above it
require World ID authorization.

<Note>
AgentBank does not take custody of your wallet funds. Payment and banking
partners may receive or process funds while completing a supported route.
</Note>

automatically, credits the mock tokens, and leaves the wallet ready for other
supported staging intents.
