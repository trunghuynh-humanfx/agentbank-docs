Connect AgentBank to ChatGPT or Claude to collect payments, exchange currencies,
send money through supported routes, and track payments from your conversation.
Describe what you want in plain language; the assistant selects the tools and
asks you to confirm payment details before creating a payment.

Remote MCP connects over HTTP and uses OAuth to identify your AgentBank account.
Each connection is scoped to the person who signs in. It uses the same AgentBank
account data, recipients, payment records, verification state, and payment rails
as the local MCP, within the permissions granted to that connection.

## Connect your account

Use this Remote MCP server URL:

```text
https://plugin.agentbank.world/mcp
```

You do not need to run the local MCP package, configure a local credential
profile, or use local agent onboarding for a Remote MCP connection. Follow the
client guide to authorize AgentBank in your browser.

## Verify the connection

After signing in, ask:

```text
Check which AgentBank account is connected, what you can access, and whether my wallet is ready. Do not create a payment.
```

The assistant checks:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the identity">
    Call `whoami` and confirm the expected owner and hosted connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check permissions">
    Call `check_my_scopes` instead of guessing which tools are authorized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the wallet">
    Call `list_wallets`. If the owner wallet is absent, finish the
    AgentBank/Privy wallet setup before attempting a wallet-funded payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do

| Task | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Preview a payment or exchange | A quote showing the amounts, fees, route, recipient outcome, and expiry before you confirm. An estimate does not reserve a rate or move money. |
| Send or collect money | A payment approval when required, then the current bank, QR, or crypto funding instruction. |
| Review several payments | A payment plan with every recipient, amount, fee, and total, followed by one combined World ID approval when required. |
| Track a payment | A progress card after funds move, with the final outcome checked against AgentBank's payment record. |
| Authorize limited crypto funding | An optional spending grant with limits you review and activate separately. |

Supported clients can render embedded AgentBank cards for these steps. Complete
World ID approval, wallet signing, and grant activation through the card or its
first-party action page. Never paste wallet secrets, OAuth tokens, or World ID
proofs into chat. If a card is unavailable, ask the assistant to reopen it or
provide the available action link.

## Try a request

```text
Show me the supported ways to collect a customer payment into my AgentBank wallet.
```

```text
Estimate sending 100 USDC to my saved bank recipient. Show the recipient amount, fees, route, and expiry before I confirm.
```

```text
Help me prepare a payment plan for my saved recipients. Review every payment and the totals with me before submission.
```

The assistant checks live routes and your account's readiness before proceeding.
Availability depends on the currency, amount, destination, and verification state.

## Approval and funding

Review the source amount, destination amount, fees, route, recipient, and expiry
before confirming a payment. For a plan, review and confirm all payments together.

Opening a funding instruction with `continue_payment` does not move money.
Complete the fiat instruction yourself, or explicitly authorize an eligible
crypto funding action. An active spending grant still requires a separate choice
to fund the current instruction; it cannot fund fiat instructions or swaps.

The assistant uses `get_payment` as the source of truth. A transfer receipt,
transaction hash, or card display alone does not prove completion: the payment
must report `completed`.

## Disconnect

Revoke the hosted connection from AgentBank connection settings. You can also
remove the plugin or connector from your AI client's settings. Remote MCP does
not expose the local `begin_agent_onboarding`, `relogin`, `revoke_agent`, or
local wallet-execution tools.

For a device-based setup, see [Connection types](/getting-started/connection-types).
To use AgentBank's own chat without an external AI client, open
[app.agentbank.world](https://app.agentbank.world).
