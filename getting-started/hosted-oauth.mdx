---
title: "Hosted OAuth"
description: "Use AgentBank through an OAuth-connected agent with embedded payment cards."
---

A hosted OAuth connection identifies the signed-in AgentBank user without a
local installation credential. Connect AgentBank from the OAuth-capable host,
complete the AgentBank authorization screen, and then begin with these checks:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the identity">
    Call `whoami` and confirm the expected owner and hosted connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check permissions">
    Call `check_my_scopes` instead of guessing which tools are authorized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the wallet">
    Call `list_wallets`. If the owner wallet is absent, finish the
    AgentBank/Privy wallet setup before attempting a wallet-funded payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use hosted payment cards">
    Approval, funding, grant activation, and progress are displayed in
    dedicated cards. Do not repeat card links in chat unless the user asks or
    reports that a card is unavailable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Hosted OAuth does not expose `begin_agent_onboarding`, `relogin`,
`revoke_agent`, or local wallet execution. Revoke a hosted connection from
the connection settings in AgentBank.
