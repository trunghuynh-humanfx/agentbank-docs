---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Connect AgentBank MCP, authorize your account, install the skill, and verify readiness."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an environment">
    Use [staging](/getting-started/environment#staging) for mock tokens or
    [production](/getting-started/environment#production) for live routes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the AgentBank MCP">
    Follow the configuration for your client in
    [Connect your agent](/getting-started/connect-your-agent).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the AgentBank Pay skill">
    Install from `trunghuynh-humanfx/agentbank-docs` or load the canonical skill
    URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check readiness">
    Ask your agent:

    ```text
    Check my AgentBank connection, account readiness, and wallet. Do not create a payment.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete browser authorization">
    If credentials are missing, your agent returns a URL titled **Connect to
    your AgentBank Account**. Sign in and approve the connected agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the account">
    Your agent checks `whoami`, scopes, account status, and `list_wallets`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
