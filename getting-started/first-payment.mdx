---
title: "Your first payment"
description: "Ask for a payment, review the quote, approve it, and track the result."
---

Start in [AgentBank chat](https://app.agentbank.world) or with a
[connected assistant](/getting-started/quickstart).

```text
Help me send money to a bank account. Show me how much I will pay, how much they will receive, and all fees before I confirm.
```

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the payment">
    Tell the agent the currencies, amount, and destination country. Say whether
    you want to send a specific amount or have the recipient receive a specific
    amount. The agent checks availability and asks for any missing details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Choose a saved recipient or provide the requested bank, wallet, or QR
    details. Review the recipient, amount sent, amount received, fees, payment
    route, and quote expiry. An estimate does not move money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and fund">
    Complete World ID approval if requested. Then follow the payment's funding
    instructions or confirm the supported wallet action. Opening an instruction
    or approving a payment does not by itself fund it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Ask the agent to track the payment until AgentBank confirms completion.
    If the payment is still pending, continue tracking the same payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the quote changes or expires, review the new amounts and fees before
confirming again. Bank transfers and crypto transactions may become
irreversible after funds move.

See [Payment status and tracking](/payments/track-payments) for pending payments,
or [Cancel or correct a payment](/payments/cancel-or-correct) if details need to change.
