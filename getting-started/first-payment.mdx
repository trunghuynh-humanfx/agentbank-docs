---
title: "4. Your first payment"
description: "Learn the universal AgentBank payment sequence without committing to a specific corridor."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check readiness">
    Confirm the connected agent, scopes, verification, market readiness, and
    wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discover supported assets and routes">
    Use live currencies and quote-book pairs. Never assume a route exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Estimate the complete payment">
    Choose exact source or exact destination and include an explicit settlement
    asset for a two-step route. Estimates do not accept recipients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve the recipient">
    For a fiat destination, choose one instrument from the estimate's
    `recipient_requirements`, then select or create the final recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review material details">
    Show the full recipient, source and destination amounts, all fees and fee
    currencies, route, expiry, and expected browser action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply authorization policy">
    Follow `approval_ready` or `approval_required` as returned. Never infer a
    fixed threshold from the amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and continue one durable payment">
    Use stable request IDs and follow only the current Core-owned instruction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track final status">
    Poll `get_payment` until `completed`, `cancelled`, `expired`,
    `funding_timeout`, or `failed`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Do not treat an estimate ID as durable—estimates have no ID. Do not treat a
transaction hash as final payment completion.
</Warning>
