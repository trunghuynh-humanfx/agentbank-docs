---
title: "Send a bank payout"
description: "Convert supported crypto into fiat for a validated bank recipient."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Resolve the recipient">
    Search saved recipients or create one using the live rail's required bank
    fields. Use the canonical result returned by AgentBank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a direct off-ramp">
    Check live quote-book pairs for the source crypto and destination fiat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Estimate the outcome">
    Show the source amount, destination amount, all fees, full recipient,
    route, and expiry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and authorize">
    Apply the configured platform threshold. Above-threshold payments require
    World ID authorization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute the current instruction">
    Call `continue_payment`, check the wallet balance, and use
    `execute_payment_instruction` with its stable request ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track delivery">
    Poll `get_payment`. A transaction receipt alone is not bank delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Never construct calldata, choose a different spender, or substitute a recipient
after payment creation.
