---
title: "Send a bank payout"
description: "Convert supported crypto into fiat for a validated bank recipient."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a direct off-ramp">
    Check live quote-book pairs for the source crypto and destination fiat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Estimate the outcome">
    Estimate without a recipient. Read the returned `recipient_requirements`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve the recipient">
    Let the user choose a supported instrument, then search saved recipients or
    create one using its required fields. Use AgentBank's canonical result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and authorize">
    Show the complete recipient, amounts, fees, route, and expiry. Follow the
    returned approval status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute the current instruction">
    Call `continue_payment`. Local stdio checks the wallet balance and uses
    `execute_payment_instruction`; hosted OAuth uses manual card funding or a
    separately confirmed compatible spending grant for a crypto deposit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track delivery">
    Poll `get_payment`. A transaction receipt alone is not bank delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Never construct calldata, choose a different spender, or substitute a recipient
after payment creation.
