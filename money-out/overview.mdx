---
title: "Money out"
description: "Send supported crypto value to fiat bank recipients."
---

AgentBank money-out journeys convert supported crypto into fiat delivered to a
validated bank recipient.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bank payout" href="/money-out/bank-payout">
    Use a live direct off-ramp when the source asset has one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Crypto to fiat" href="/money-out/crypto-to-fiat">
    Use an explicit settlement asset when the source token lacks a direct pair.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manage recipients" href="/money-out/manage-recipients">
    Create recipients from bank fields, pasted text, QR content, or QR images.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
Bank transfers may become irreversible. Validate the complete recipient and
review the route before creating the payment.
</Warning>
