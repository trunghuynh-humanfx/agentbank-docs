---
title: "Add money to your wallet"
description: "Use a supported fiat on-ramp to fund the AgentBank wallet."
---

Use this journey when the account owner wants to convert supported fiat into a
crypto asset held by the bound AgentBank wallet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the wallet and account">
    Call `get_account_status` and `list_wallets`. Use the wallet returned by
    AgentBank; never infer or request a private key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a live on-ramp">
    Use `list_currencies` and `list_quote_book_pairs` to select a supported
    fiat-to-crypto pair.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Estimate the payment">
    Call `estimate_payment` with the bound wallet as the crypto recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and authorize">
    Apply the configured threshold policy, then call `create_payment` with the
    reviewed route and stable request ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue and fund">
    In production, give the payer the returned order-specific bank or QR
    instruction. In staging, the supported on-ramp completes automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track and verify balance">
    Poll `get_payment` until terminal, then call `get_wallet_balances`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Do not create another on-ramp merely because payment detection is pending.
Resume the existing durable payment.
</Warning>
