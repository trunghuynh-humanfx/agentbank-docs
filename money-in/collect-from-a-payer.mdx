---
title: "Collect from another payer"
description: "Create and track a one-time collection that someone else can pay."
---

The payer may be different from the AgentBank account owner. The connected
agent creates and tracks the payment for the owner, while the payer completes
the fiat instruction.

## Current flow

1. Select the destination AgentBank wallet and crypto asset.
2. Estimate the fiat-to-crypto amount and fees.
3. Create one durable payment.
4. Continue it to obtain the current fiat instruction.
5. Share only the order-specific bank account or QR instructions with the payer.
6. Poll `get_payment` and report whether it was paid.

<Warning>
Paid status does not verify payer identity. Do not claim to know who paid, and
do not treat the result as customer identity verification.
</Warning>
