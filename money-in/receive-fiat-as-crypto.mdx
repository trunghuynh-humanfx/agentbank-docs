---
title: "Receive fiat as crypto"
description: "Convert a supported fiat payment into crypto in the AgentBank wallet."
---

This is a direct on-ramp where fiat is the source and crypto is the destination.

## Information your agent needs

- source fiat currency;
- exact-source or exact-destination amount;
- destination crypto ticker and chain;
- destination AgentBank wallet;
- optional routing preference.

The agent must verify the asset using `list_currencies`, find a live pair, and
call `estimate_payment`. Before creation, it presents both amounts, every fee
and fee currency, the wallet, route, and expiry.

In production, give the payer the order-specific bank or QR instruction. In
staging, a supported on-ramp automatically credits mock tokens.

<Note>
The payer may be someone other than the wallet owner, but the agent can track
only the paid state—not payer identity.
</Note>
