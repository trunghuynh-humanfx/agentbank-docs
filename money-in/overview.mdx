Pay in a supported local currency and receive crypto in your linked AgentBank
wallet. Choose the guide based on who is paying.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Add money" href="/money-in/add-money">
    Use your own money to buy crypto for your wallet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collect from a payer" href="/money-in/collect-from-a-payer">
    Give someone else payment instructions or an available shareable link.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Your assistant checks live availability and shows the amount, fees, destination,
and expiry before you confirm. AgentBank can report whether a collection was
paid, but your assistant cannot identify its payer.
