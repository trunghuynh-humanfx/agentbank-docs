"Collect money" can describe three AgentBank journeys:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Add money" href="/money-in/add-money">
    On-ramp fiat into your own AgentBank wallet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collect from a payer" href="/money-in/collect-from-a-payer">
    Create an order that another person can pay using its bank or QR details.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Receive fiat as crypto" href="/money-in/receive-fiat-as-crypto">
    Accept a supported fiat source and receive a selected crypto asset.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

These journeys may use the same direct on-ramp tools, but the payer and user
experience differ.

<Note>
Shareable single-payment links are coming soon. Today, give the payer the
order-specific bank account or QR instructions returned by AgentBank.
</Note>

AgentBank can tell your agent whether the order was paid. It does not currently
identify who paid it.
