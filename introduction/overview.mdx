title: "What AgentBank does"
description: "Understand what AgentBank provides to users, builders, and AI agents."
---

AgentBank connects authorized AI agents to supported fiat payment rails and
on-chain settlement through MCP.

An agent can discover currencies and live pairs, estimate a complete payment,
resolve recipients, create a durable payment, follow Core-generated
instructions, and track final status. Supported journeys include direct
on-ramps, direct off-ramps, same-chain swaps, fiat-to-fiat routes, and
token-to-fiat routes.

## Control boundaries

- The account and AgentBank wallet are tied to the user's login.
- Privy provides wallet infrastructure.
- AgentBank Core remains authoritative for authorization, route locking,
  payment instructions, transaction verification, and durable status.
- KYC, World ID payment authorization, and AgentKit verification are separate
  mechanisms with different purposes.
