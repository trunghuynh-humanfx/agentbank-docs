---
title: "Why AgentBank"
description: "See how AgentBank turns fragmented payment requirements into agent-ready workflows."
---

Stablecoins make digital value transferable, but an AI agent still needs a
safe way to collect local fiat, convert assets, and pay bank recipients.

Local rails differ by currency, country, recipient fields, KYC requirements,
eligibility, fees, expiry, and settlement behavior. A model should not guess
those rules or assemble an arbitrary transaction.

AgentBank exposes high-level payment journeys through MCP:

1. Discover the live currencies and route pairs.
2. Validate the recipient and requested amount.
3. Estimate the complete executable outcome.
4. Apply the user's configured authorization policy.
5. Create one durable payment and follow its current instruction.
6. Track the aggregate payment until AgentBank reports a terminal state.

This gives agents useful payment capability while keeping authorization,
route selection, wallet execution, and recovery inside defined boundaries.
