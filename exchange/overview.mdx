title: "Exchange"
description: "Discover quotes, compare complete outcomes, and execute supported conversions."
---

AgentBank separates route discovery from durable execution:

- `browse_quote_book` shows anonymous rough quote bands.
- `get_ramp_quote` creates a direct on-ramp or off-ramp quote intent.
- `estimate_payment` evaluates a complete supported end-to-end payment.
- `create_payment` locks the selected route into durable state.
