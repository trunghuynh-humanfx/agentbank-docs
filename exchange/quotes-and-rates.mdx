---
title: "Quotes and rates"
description: "Compare anonymous offers using complete executable outcomes, not headline rates alone."
---

Use `list_quote_book_pairs` as the live source of direct corridors and
`browse_quote_book` for rough anonymous bands.

When comparing routes, evaluate:

- source and destination amounts;
- percentage and flat fees;
- each fee currency;
- effective output;
- expiry;
- rail and eligibility requirements;
- route steps;
- the user's routing preference.

Partner identity is intentionally omitted. Do not ask the agent to expose or
choose a partner by name.

<Note>
Use `get_ramp_quote` only for direct on-ramp or off-ramp discovery. Use
`estimate_payment` for complete payments and crypto swaps.
</Note>
