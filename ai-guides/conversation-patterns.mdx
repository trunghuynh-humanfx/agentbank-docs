---
title: "Conversation patterns"
description: "Use clear task prompts for common AgentBank workflows."
---

## Check connection

```text
Check whether AgentBank is connected, show account and wallet readiness, and do not create a payment.
```

## Fund staging

```text
Find a supported staging on-ramp, complete it for mock tokens, and show my resulting wallet balance.
```

## Collect fiat from another payer

```text
Create a one-time fiat collection into my AgentBank wallet. Show me the complete amount, fees, and expiry before creation, then give me the order-specific bank or QR instructions to share with the payer.
```

## Pay a bank recipient

```text
Pay my saved recipient in VND from supported crypto. Estimate the route, show all material details, apply my authorization threshold, and track final delivery.
```

## Track or recover

```text
Read the current durable state of payment pay_..., tell me the next safe action, and do not create a duplicate payment.
```

These patterns show observable goals and actions. They do not expose or request
hidden chain-of-thought.
